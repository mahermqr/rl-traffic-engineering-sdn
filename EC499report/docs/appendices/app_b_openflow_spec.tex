\section{OpenFlow 1.3 Common Header Specification}
All OpenFlow 1.3 protocol messages begin with an 8-byte common header. The binary wire format is detailed in Table~\ref{tab:ofp_header_format}.

\begin{table}[htbp]
\centering
\caption{OpenFlow 1.3 Common Header Binary Structure}
\label{tab:ofp_header_format}
\small
\begin{tabularx}{\textwidth}{c c c X}
\toprule
\textbf{Byte Offset} & \textbf{Field Name} & \textbf{Width} & \textbf{Description \& Wire Value} \\
\midrule
0 & \texttt{version} & 8 bits & Protocol version. For OpenFlow 1.3, wire value is \texttt{0x04}. \\
1 & \texttt{type} & 8 bits & Message type code (e.g., \texttt{OFPT\_PACKET\_IN = 10}). \\
2--3 & \texttt{length} & 16 bits & Total length of message including header in bytes. \\
4--7 & \texttt{xid} & 32 bits & Transaction ID associated with this message for matching requests to replies. \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabularx}
\end{table}

\section{Core OpenFlow 1.3 Message Type Enumeration}
Table~\ref{tab:ofp_types_enumeration} lists the primary message types defined in ONF TS-006 \cite{onf2012openflow} utilized by our platform.

\begin{table}[htbp]
\centering
\caption{Primary OpenFlow 1.3 Message Types Utilized in the Platform}
\label{tab:ofp_types_enumeration}
\small
\begin{tabularx}{\textwidth}{c l c X}
\toprule
\textbf{Code} & \textbf{Message Constant} & \textbf{Direction} & \textbf{Operational Function} \\
\midrule
0 & \texttt{OFPT\_HELLO} & Symmetric & Negotiates protocol version during TCP connection setup. \\
2 & \texttt{OFPT\_ECHO\_REQUEST} & Symmetric & Probes connection liveness and active Echo-RTT latency. \\
3 & \texttt{OFPT\_ECHO\_REPLY} & Symmetric & Responds to echo request with identical payload. \\
5 & \texttt{OFPT\_FEATURES\_REQUEST} & Controller $\to$ Switch & Queries switch DPID, buffer count, and table capabilities. \\
6 & \texttt{OFPT\_FEATURES\_REPLY} & Switch $\to$ Controller & Reports datapath ID and supported OpenFlow capabilities. \\
10 & \texttt{OFPT\_PACKET\_IN} & Switch $\to$ Controller & Transfers unclassified packet header/miss to controller. \\
13 & \texttt{OFPT\_PACKET\_OUT} & Controller $\to$ Switch & Instructs switch to output packet out of specific port(s). \\
14 & \texttt{OFPT\_FLOW\_MOD} & Controller $\to$ Switch & Installs, modifies, or deletes flow table entries in TCAM. \\
15 & \texttt{OFPT\_GROUP\_MOD} & Controller $\to$ Switch & Manages OpenFlow Group tables (Select / Fast Failover). \\
18 & \texttt{OFPT\_MULTIPART\_REQUEST} & Controller $\to$ Switch & Polls port stats, flow counters, or table descriptions. \\
19 & \texttt{OFPT\_MULTIPART\_REPLY} & Switch $\to$ Controller & Delivers multi-part port octets and packet counters. \\
20 & \texttt{OFPT\_BARRIER\_REQUEST} & Controller $\to$ Switch & Enforces sequential execution of preceding FlowMod rules. \\
21 & \texttt{OFPT\_BARRIER\_REPLY} & Switch $\to$ Controller & Confirms all prior FlowMod rules are committed to TCAM. \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabularx}
\end{table}

\section{Annotated OpenFlow Hex Packet Dumps}

\subsection{Annotated \texttt{OFPPacketIn} Wire Frame}
Listing~\ref{lst:packet_in_hex} displays the byte-accurate wire format of an OpenFlow 1.3.5 \texttt{OFPPacketIn} message captured during switch table-miss ingress, formatted in 8-byte rows strictly adhering to ONF TS-006 specifications.

\begin{lstlisting}[basicstyle=\ttfamily\scriptsize, caption={Byte-Accurate Hex Dump of OFPPacketIn Message (OpenFlow 1.3.5)}, label={lst:packet_in_hex}]
# --- OFP Header (8 bytes: ver, type, len, xid) ---
04 0a 00 60 00 00 00 07   # Ver: 0x04 (OF 1.3), Type: 0x0a (PACKET_IN), Len: 96B, XID: 7
# --- OFP Packet-In Body (16 bytes: buffer_id, total_len, reason, table_id, cookie) ---
ff ff ff ff 00 36 01 00   # Buffer ID: 0xffffffff (NO_BUFFER), Total Len: 54B, Reason: 0x01 (MISS), Table: 0
00 00 00 00 00 00 00 01   # Cookie: 0x0000000000000001
# --- OFP Match (16 bytes: type, len, OXM_OF_IN_PORT, padding) ---
00 01 00 0c 80 00 00 04   # Type: 1 (OXM), Len: 12B | OXM Basic (0x8000), Field: IN_PORT (0x00), Len: 4
00 00 00 02 00 00 00 00   # Port: 2 (0x00000002) | 4-byte 64-bit alignment padding
00 00                     # 2-byte structure pad before encapsulated frame
# --- Encapsulated Ethernet Frame (IPv4 / TCP SYN, 54 bytes) ---
00 00 00 00 00 02 00 00   # Dst MAC: 00:00:00:00:00:02 (h2) | Src MAC [0:2]
00 00 00 01 08 00 45 00   # Src MAC [2:6]: 00:00:00:01 (h1) | EtherType: 0x0800 (IPv4) | IPv4 Ver/IHL/DSCP
00 28 00 01 00 00 40 06   # IPv4 Len: 40B, ID: 1, Flags/Frag: 0, TTL: 64, Proto: 6 (TCP)
7c c0 0a 00 00 01 0a 00   # Checksum, Src IP: 10.0.0.1 (h1) | Dst IP [0:2]
00 02 1f 90 00 50 00 00   # Dst IP [2:4]: 10.0.0.2 (h2) | Src Port: 8080 (0x1f90), Dst Port: 80 (0x0050)
00 01 00 00 00 00 50 02   # TCP Seq: 1, Ack: 0, Offset: 5 (20B), Flags: 0x02 (SYN)
20 00 00 00 00 00         # Window: 8192, Checksum, Urgent Pointer
\end{lstlisting}

\subsection{Annotated \texttt{OFPFlowMod} Installation Wire Frame}
Listing~\ref{lst:flow_mod_hex} displays the byte-accurate wire format of an OpenFlow 1.3.5 \texttt{OFPFlowMod} message pushed by the controller to install a Priority 100 forwarding rule with an \texttt{OFPAT\_OUTPUT} action.

\begin{lstlisting}[basicstyle=\ttfamily\scriptsize, caption={Byte-Accurate Hex Dump of OFPFlowMod Installation Message (OpenFlow 1.3.5)}, label={lst:flow_mod_hex}]
# --- OFP Header (8 bytes: ver, type, len, xid) ---
04 0e 00 68 00 00 00 08   # Ver: 0x04 (OF 1.3), Type: 0x0e (FLOW_MOD), Len: 104B, XID: 8
# --- OFP Flow-Mod Body (40 bytes) ---
00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00   # Cookie: 0x0000000000000000
00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00   # Cookie Mask: 0x0000000000000000
00 00 00 1e 01 2c 00 64   # Table: 0, Command: 0 (ADD), Idle: 30s (0x001e), Hard: 300s (0x012c), Prio: 100
ff ff ff ff ff ff ff ff   # Buffer ID: 0xffffffff (NONE), Out Port: 0xffffffff (ANY)
ff ff ff ff 00 00 00 00   # Out Group: 0xffffffff (ANY), Flags: 0x0000, 2-byte pad
# --- Match Fields (OXM, 32 bytes including padding) ---
00 01 00 1a 80 00 0a 02   # Type: 1 (OXM), Len: 26B | OXM eth_type (0x8000/0a), Len: 2
08 00 80 00 16 04 0a 00   # EtherType: 0x0800 | OXM ipv4_src (0x8000/16), Len: 4, IP [0:2]
00 01 80 00 18 04 0a 00   # IP [2:4]: 10.0.0.1 | OXM ipv4_dst (0x8000/18), Len: 4, IP [0:2]
00 02 00 00 00 00 00 00   # IP [2:4]: 10.0.0.2 | 6-byte 64-bit alignment padding
# --- Instructions (APPLY_ACTIONS: Output to Port 3, 24 bytes) ---
00 04 00 18 00 00 00 00   # Inst Type: 0x0004 (APPLY_ACTIONS), Len: 24B, 4-byte pad
00 00 00 10 00 00 00 03   # Action Type: 0x0000 (OUTPUT), Len: 16B, Port: 3 (0x00000003)
00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00   # Max Length: 0 (Send full frame), 6-byte pad
\end{lstlisting}
