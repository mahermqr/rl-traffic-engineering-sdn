🧠 Autonomous D3QN Traffic Engineering

Synapse is a carrier-grade closed-loop SDN Traffic Engineering system driven by a Dueling Double Deep Q-Network (D3QN) policy engine running directly in a Ryu OpenFlow 1.3 controller.

Unlike static shortest-path protocols (OSPF RFC 2328, Dijkstra) or stateless hashing (ECMP) which cause severe hot-spot congestion and buffer drops during asymmetric traffic surges, Synapse observes live port statistics, link delays, and queue depths to proactively steer flows onto high-bandwidth, low-jitter detour paths.

🖱️ Interacting with the Topology Canvas
  • Zoom: Scroll your mouse wheel over the canvas or use the ➕ / ➖ buttons in the header to zoom from 40% to 400%.
  • Pan: Click and drag anywhere on the canvas to inspect large topologies (e.g. 14-node GEANT, NSFNET Continental core).
  • Switch Selection: Click on any switch node on the canvas to set it as the Source, then click another switch to set it as the Destination. Route prediction triggers automatically!
  • Live Link Badges: Each link displays its real-time bandwidth utilization percentage pill (green < 50%, amber 50-80%, red > 80%).
  • Fault Overlays: Severed links are rendered with red dashed lines and ⚡ CUT markers; degraded links are marked in amber.
  • Overlay Trails: Active D3QN calculated routes are highlighted with violet/emerald path lines. OSPF shortest paths are shown with dashed amber lines.
Peak Bottleneck Utilization (U_max) max_{(u,v)} (Load / Capacity)
The highest single link utilization in the entire SDN graph. Lower bottleneck load is the primary objective of Traffic Engineering, preventing buffer overflows and packet drops.
Jitter (Delay Variance) σ(Delay) = sqrt(E[(D - μ)^2])
Standard deviation of packet transit times across all active flow paths. Critical for real-time video streaming, VoIP, and financial low-latency protocols.
Jain's Fairness Index (Σ x_i)^2 / (N * Σ x_i^2)
Evaluates whether fabric capacity is distributed equitably across all competing edge flows (ranges from 1/N to 1.0, where 1.0 indicates perfect fairness).
QoS SLA Compliance Score 100 - (Loss% * 500) - (Delay_excess * 10)
Compound metric measuring the percentage of active dynamic flows strictly meeting carrier-grade service level agreements (latency < 15 ms, loss < 0.1%).
